=== Root Cause Analysis ===
Primary failure: insufficient trade count (26 IS trades over 15 years).
Monthly rebalancing between TLT/SHY produces only ~1-2 switches per year because
the yield curve slope persists in one regime for extended periods. This low-frequency
design fails Gate 1 minimum trade floor (100) by 4x.

Contributing failures:
  1. Low IS Sharpe (0.58) — monthly signal with only 26 trades has high per-trade
     variance. The high win rate (65%) and profit factor (5.8) suggest selectivity,
     not systematic alpha.
  2. OOS collapse (-0.185 Sharpe, -33% MDD) — 2019-2024 includes curve inversions
     (2019, 2022-2023) that contradict the hypothesis: spread goes negative but TLT
     underperforms despite the "SHORT_DURATION" signal executing correctly.
  3. Permutation test fails (p=0.498) — monthly resampling with 26 trades cannot
     reject the null of random alpha at any reasonable significance level.
  4. Walk-forward 0/4 — extreme OOS variance (std=0.90) across windows indicates
     regime-dependent performance, not robust structural alpha.

=== Recommendation ===
REJECT — do not advance to paper trading. Return to Engineering Director.
Strategy is fundamentally under-traded for Gate 1 statistical requirements.
Bond carry as a MONTHLY signal on TLT/SHY is too low-frequency to satisfy
the >=100 trade minimum and <=20% win-rate variance needed for significance.

Potential paths forward (for Engineering Director / Research Director consideration):
  - Redesign at weekly or daily frequency (50+ signals/year)
  - Add multi-asset bond universe (TLT, IEF, SHY, TIP) to increase trade frequency
  - Test yield-curve slope as a REGIME FILTER layered on top of another strategy
    rather than as a standalone duration-timing signal
