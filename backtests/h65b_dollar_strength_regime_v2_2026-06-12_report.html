v2.0 Design Notes

  • Asymmetric exit: Both DXY_mom < threshold AND VIX < vix_threshold required to exit risk-off. Prevents Q4 2022 premature re-entry.
  • 2014-2015 fix: VIX filter prevents risk-off entry when DXY strong but VIX calm (VIX was mostly 10-15 in 2014-2015).
  • asymmetric_exit=True is NOT a free parameter — it is required design. Do not test symmetric version.
  • Sticky-state benefit: Fewer transitions vs symmetric version; lower transaction cost drag.
